# Acme Demo Co. — Customer Handbook (Fictional Sample)

> This is fictional sample content for a RAG demo. Any resemblance to a real
> company is coincidental.

## Refunds and Returns
Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of purchase. To be eligible,
the item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are processed to
the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days** after we receive the
returned item. Shipping fees are non-refundable except when the return is due to
our error.

## Password Reset
To reset your password, go to the login page and click **"Forgot password"**.
Enter the email associated with your account and you will receive a reset link
that is valid for **60 minutes**. If the link expires, simply request a new one.
For security, we never send your existing password by email.

## Support Hours
Customer support is available **Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (CST)**.
Weekend support is limited to email only, with responses on the next business
day. Priority phone support is available to Premium plan customers 24/7.

## Loyalty Program
Any customer with an active account is eligible for the **Acme Rewards** loyalty
program. Members earn **1 point per dollar** spent and can redeem **100 points
for a $5 credit**. Points expire **12 months** after they are earned. Premium
plan members earn points at **double the standard rate**.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes **3 to 5 business days** within the continental US.
Expedited shipping (**1 to 2 business days**) is available at checkout for an
additional fee. We currently do not ship to international addresses.

## Data Privacy
We store only the account information necessary to provide the service. Customer
data is never sold to third parties. You can request deletion of your account
and associated data at any time by contacting support.
